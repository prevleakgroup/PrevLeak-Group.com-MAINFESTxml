1. Executive Summary

The PrevLeak Group™ ecosystem brings together infrastructure, design, and mobility concepts under a single technical strategy. This site presents the core platform overview in a publication-friendly format.

2. Technical Architecture

The architecture is organized around functional system behavior and product presentation, with emphasis on resilient deployment and modular delivery.

Functional Design

  • Hermetic isolation: Modular compartments protect sensitive components from environmental exposure.
  • Sensor fusion: Integrated sensing supports continuous monitoring and environmental awareness.
  • Dual-link connectivity: Network fallback paths maintain service continuity across link failures.

Aesthetic Design

  • Material profile: Composite construction balances durability, cost, and maintainability.
  • Recessed housing: Flush geometry supports a clean exterior while preserving hardware performance.
