2. Technical Architecture: F & A Patent Pillars

Our core intellectual property is structurally divided into Functional (F) and Aesthetic (A) designations under CIPC frameworks to optimize statutory market protection.

Functional Design (F2025/00821)

  • Hermetic Isolation: Internal modular compartments sealed completely to safeguard electronics against atmospheric degradation and corrosive sewer dynamics.
  • Sensor Fusion Array: Integrated multi-sensor arrays incorporating continuous ultrasonic depth tracking and 360-degree environmental variables.
  • Dual-Link Transceivers: Low-power operational network profiles with automated fallbacks switching between LPWAN protocols and direct LEO Satellite downlinks.

Aesthetic Design (A2025/00820)

  • Structural Material Profile: Highly specialized composite geometric molding utilizing low-scrap value industrial components to eliminate infrastructure vandalism incentives.
  • Recessed Component Housing: Flush physical geometries allowing external antenna arrays to perform optimal network operations without structurally weakening the heavy-load surface frame.
